1 · Paper holds, tubes speak

Two materials only. Paper is static, editorial, mostly white space. Tubes are the only places that glow, animate, or scanline. Never put a tube inside a tube.

2 · Everything is operable from the keypad

Every page has a soft-key bar. Number keys 1–9 always work. The mouse is optional; the fun is pressing keys.

3 · One blue, used like a cursor

OSD blue marks "you are here" and the live screen. Amber means something is live or moving. Red pen belongs to the archivist, never to buttons.

4 · Labelled like equipment

Things get codes (K15, Д7, P5), sheet numbers and margin tags. Copy is dry and procedural, with jokes hidden in the codes.
